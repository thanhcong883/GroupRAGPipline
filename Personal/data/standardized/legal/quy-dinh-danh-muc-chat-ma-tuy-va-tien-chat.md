Dieu 1. Danh muc chat ma tiy va tien chat
Ban hanh kem theo Nghi dinh nay Phu luc cac danh muc chat ma tuy va
tién chat sau day:
Danh muc I: Cac chat ma tiy tuyet doi cam sur dung trong y hoc va doi song
xa hoi; viec sur dung cac chat nay trong nghien curu, kiem nghiem, giam dinh, vi
muc dich quoc phong, an ninh theo quy dinh dac biet cia co quan co tham quyen:
IA. Cac chat gay nghien va muoi, dong phan, ester, ether va muoi cia cac
dong phan, ester, ether co the ton tai cia cac chat sau (trir khi ester, ether cua
chat do nam trong danh muc khac);
IB. Cac chat huong than va muoi, dong phan, muoi cua dong phan co thé
ton tai cua cac chat sau.
Danh muc II: Cac chat ma tiy dugc su dung han che trong nghien ciru,
kiem nghiem, giam dinh, vi muc dich quoc phong, an ninh hoac trong linh vuc
y te theo quy dinh cua co quan co tham quyen:
IIA. Cac chat gay nghien va muoi, dong phan, ester, ether, muoi cua cac
dong phan, ester, ether co the ton tai cua cac chat sau (triur khi ester, ether cia
chat do nam trong danh muc khac);

IIB. Cac chat huong than va muoi, dong phan, muoi cua dong phan co thé
ton tai cia cac chat sau.
Danh muc II: Cac .chat ma tuy dugc si dung trong nghien ciru, kiem
nghiem, giam dinh, vi muc dich quoc phong, an ninh hoac trong linh vuc y te,
thu y theo quy dinh cua co quan co tham quyén:
Cac chat huong than va muoi, dong phan, muoi cua dong phan co the ton
tai cua cac chat sau.
Danh muc IV: Cac tien chat:
IVA: Cac tien chat thiét yeéu, tham gia vao cau truc chat ma tuy, khong co
ung dung hop phap trong y hoc va doi song xa hoi, dugc sir dung han che trong
nghien ciru, kiem nghiem, giam dinh, vi muc dich quoc phong, an ninh theo
quy dinh dac biet cia co quan co tham quyen;
IVB: Cac tien chat thiét yeu, tham gia vao cau triuc chat ma tuy, co ung
dung hop phap trong y hoc va doi song xa hoi;
IVC: Cac tien chat la hoa chat, dung moi, chat xuc tac dung trong qua trinh
san xuat chat ma tiy.

nghiep va moi truong.

Ghi chi chung cac danh muc
1. Ma so CAS
- Chat ma tuy dang nguyen lieu thuc vat khong co ma thong tin CAS, mot so chat
ma tiy chua co san ma CAS & thoi diem cap nhat danh muc.
- Ma CAS cua cac chat trong danh muc khong phai la duy nhat. Ma CAS trong
danh muc thuong la dang base hay dang hon hop racemic cua chat. Bat ky dang muoi,
ester, ether, dong phan, muoi cia dong phan cua mot chat trong danh muc neu ton tai,
du ma CAS khong xuat hien trong danh muc van dugc coi la mot chat bi kiem soat.
2. Dong phan
2.1. Khi mot chat co cac dong phan lap the (stereochemical) ma ten khoa hoc cua
chat hién thi dang hon hop doi quang (+) (racemic):
a. Neu cau tric hoa hoc cua chat co mot tam bat doi (chiral centre) thi ca 2 dang
dong phan doi quang (enationmers) R va S (+ hoac -) va hon hop RS-racemic (+) deu bi
kiem soat.
b. Neu cau truc hoa hoc cua chat co nhieu hon mot tam bat doi thi tat ca cac dong
phan phi dbi quang (diastereoisomers) va cac cap doi quang (racemic) cua chung deu bi
kiem soat.
2.2. Khi mot chat co cac dong phan lap the, cau truc co mot tam bat doi ma ten
khoa hoc cua chat chi ghi mot loai dong phan doi quang cu the (R hoac S, + hoac -) thi
dang hon hop RS-racemic (+) cua chat do cung bi kiem soat.
2.3. Khi mot chat co cac dong phan lap the, cau truc co nhieu hon mot tam bat doi
ma ten khoa hoc cua chat chi ghi mot loai dong phan phi doi quang (diastereoisomers)
cu the thi chi co dong phan phi doi quang do bi kiem soat.
2.4. Khi mot dong phan doi quang bi kiem soat thi hon hop cia dong phan doi
quang do vdi dong phan doi quang con lai cing bi kiem soat.
